# RVA.guru Local Apps Prototype

Interactive prototype suite of 9 local Richmond, VA web apps designed in the style of [rva.guru](https://rva.guru/).

## Apps included
- ☕ Coffee & Café Map
- 🍻 Happy Hour & Patio Map
- 🌲 Parks, Trails & Outdoor Spots
- 🥬 Farmers Markets & Food Trucks
- 🚗 Commute & Parking Helper
- 📅 Events & Free Things To Do
- 🏘️ Neighborhood Vibe Explorer
- 🛍️ Local Business Finder
- 🐕 Dog-Friendly Map

Pure static HTML + Leaflet maps. Open `index.html` or visit the live deployment.

Sample data only — not affiliated with the live rva.guru site.
